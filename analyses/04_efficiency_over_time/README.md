# Question 4: Has Drafted-Specialist Efficiency Changed Over Time?

## Answer

| Cohort | Players | Mean wAV surplus | Mean games surplus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1980s | 91 | −0.135 | −0.268 |
| 1990s | 44 | −0.143 | −0.287 |
| 2000s | 46 | 0.246 | 0.484 |
| 2010–2015 | 19 | 0.383 | 0.776 |

Specialists drafted in the 2000s and early 2010s performed better relative to pick-based expectations than earlier cohorts. This suggests improved selectivity, but the small 2010s sample, era effects, and missing pre-draft quality controls prevent a causal conclusion.
