# Question 2: Do Early Specialist Selections Outperform Later Selections?

## Answer

Yes, in raw career outcomes. Among 200 kickers and punters drafted from 1980–2015:

| Round group | Players | Mean games | Mean wAV | Reached 48 games |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rounds 1–3 | 25 | 141.1 | 18.7 | 72.0% |
| Rounds 4–5 | 46 | 106.5 | 12.8 | 63.0% |
| Round 6+ | 129 | 65.6 | 8.6 | 38.0% |

Differences are statistically significant for games, wAV, and reaching 48 games. This does not prove that earlier selection causes success; teams select stronger prospects earlier.
